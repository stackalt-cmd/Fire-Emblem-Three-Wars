Fight the tyranny of the Archbishop as the Adrestian Empire or defend the "Holy Church" as the Knights of Seiros!


Journey across Fodlan and take part in some of Fire Emblem's most iconic battles, but this time in an RTS format. As rumors of war begin to formulate in the surrounding villages due to the tensions between the Church of Seiros and the Empire, the Archbishop, Lady Rhea, forms an army in secret just in case the rumors become fact, what happens next is something entirely different than what she had expected and it all began with three students being chased in the woods by bandits...


**Fire Emblem Three Wars** is a mod for _0 AD Alpha 26_, while Alpha 27 is being worked on. Currently there is not much to show, hence there being a lack of files on the official mod page (https://www.moddb.com/mods/fire-emblem-three-wars), this repository serves as a collection of all we have accomplished so far. The purpose of this repo is for those who wish to help us bring this mod to the public. For those who wish to undertake this task with us, please visit our discord server (https://discord.gg/4jFtZnwg9T) and ping "**@House Hresveld#Admin**" in either the **fodlan** or **general** chats. Thank you for visiting this page, and we hope that this mod will satisfy your expectations upon its future release on Mod DB...
